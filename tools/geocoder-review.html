Triplet — Geocoder with Review

Fetches up to 4 Nominatim candidates per place (address-only AND name-based when an address is known, merged together). The closest plausible candidate is auto-accepted automatically — no clicking required. Only places with no candidate within 12km of the city center (or no candidate at all) are left open below for you to glance at and pick manually; everything else is collapsed and already included in the SQL. Runs at 1 request per 2.5 seconds. SQL is generated automatically once fetching finishes — you can still expand any collapsed entry to override it, then regenerate.
